## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Overview

### 1.1 About 321 Clementi
**321 Clementi** is a prominent lifestyle, shopping, entertainment, and culinary destination situated in the heart of Clementi, Singapore. The brand stands as a dynamic focal point for students, families, and residents, bringing together retail, rooftop fitness, dining, and cinema under one roof.

### 1.2 Brand Essence & Pillars
- **Countdown to Excitement ("3-2-1"):** The sequential numbering embodies anticipation, energy, and an immediate start to leisure and fun.
- **Interconnected Community:** The interlocking, ribbon-like structure of the numbers represents the weaving together of diverse social groups, generations, and activities.
- **Vibrancy & Playfulness:** A spectrum of 6 distinct, harmonious hues projects vitality, friendliness, modernity, and approachability.
- **Clean Architectural Modernism:** The geometric sans-serif wordmark anchors the playful mark with civic clarity and structural elegance.

### 2.1 The Interlocking Logomark ("321")
1. **The Number '3':**
   - **Upper Arch:** Fresh Leaf Green (`#71BF45`) symbolizing nature, wellness, and vitality.
   - **Lower Loop:** Vivid Sky Cyan (`#38BEDD`) representing youth, clarity, and open spaces.
   - Overlaps seamlessly into the loop of the numeral '2'.
2. **The Number '2':**
   - **Upper Arch & Diagonal Ribbon:** Soft Wisteria Lavender (`#A87FB7`), bridging the cool tones of the '3' and the warm tones of the '1'.
   - **Horizontal Base Bar:** Vibrant Blossom Pink (`#EF5A92`), providing grounding and balance.
   - Weaves over the stem of '1' and under the curves of '3'.
3. **The Number '1':**
   - **Flag / Peak:** Deep Berry Crimson (`#ED1651`), delivering immediate focal punch at the upper apex.
   - **Vertical Stem:** Radiant Tangerine Orange (`#F47820`), providing strong vertical foundation and warmth.

### 2.2 The Wordmark ("CLEMENTI")
- **Typeface Style:** Modern Geometric Uppercase Sans-serif (high legibility, low stroke modulation, circular 'C' and 'O', sharp vertex on 'M').
- **Letter-spacing (Tracking):** Generous, open tracking (+240 tracking / `0.24em` letter-spacing) to project elegance, architectural balance, and premium neighborhood presence.
- **Color:** Exact match to the primary Crimson / Ruby Pink accent (`#ED1651`), creating an optic grounding that anchors the multi-colored numerals.

## 4. Typography System

### 4.1 Logotype & Headings: Geometric Sans
To complement the clean, circular geometry of the "321" emblem and the "CLEMENTI" wordmark:
- **Primary Type Family:** **Montserrat** / **Century Gothic** / **Futura PT**
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`

#### Hierarchy & Scale
- **Display 1 (Hero Title):** Montserrat SemiBold | `48px` / `1.15` | Tracking: `-0.02em`
- **H1 (Primary Section):** Montserrat Bold | `32px` / `1.25` | Tracking: `-0.01em`
- **H2 (Card / Subsection):** Montserrat SemiBold | `24px` / `1.3` | Tracking: `0.00em`
- **H3 (Module Headers):** Montserrat Medium | `18px` / `1.4` | Tracking: `+0.01em`
- **Wordmark Tracking Standard:** Uppercase | `tracking: 0.22em` - `0.26em`

### 4.2 Body & Functional Copy
- **Body Font:** **Inter** or **Plus Jakarta Sans**
- **Body Regular:** `15px` / `1.6` line-height | Regular 400 | Slate Grey (`#4A5568`)
- **Body Bold / Emphasized:** `15px` / `1.6` line-height | SemiBold 600 | Charcoal (`#1A202C`)
- **Caption / Meta:** `12px` / `1.4` line-height | Medium 500 | Tracking: `+0.02em`

## 5. Logo Clearspace, Sizing & Rules

### 5.1 Clear Space
The minimum clear space around all sides of the logo lockup is defined as **`X`**, where **`X` = 50% of the height of the wordmark "CLEMENTI"**.
- No text, graphics, icons, or borders may intrude into this boundary.
- Always maintain generous breathing space to let the multi-color elements resonate.

### 5.2 Minimum Reproducible Size
- **Digital Screen:** Minimum `120px` width (Stacked Lockup) | `160px` width (Horizontal Lockup).
- **Physical Print:** Minimum `25mm` width for full fidelity offset printing.
- For favicons or ultra-compact icons below `32px`, use the standalone "321" emblem without the "CLEMENTI" subtitle.

### 5.3 Approved Lockup Variations
1. **Primary Vertical Lockup:** Centered interlocking "321" over tracking-expanded "CLEMENTI" (as shown in primary image).
2. **Horizontal Lockup:** "321" mark on the left with "321 CLEMENTI" two-line or single-line wordmark to the right.
3. **Monochrome Inversion:** Solid Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) on dark photographic or deep charcoal backgrounds.
4. **Solid Brand Crimson:** Single-color `#ED1651` variant for mono-ink stationery, receipts, and embossing.

### 5.4 Brand Misuse (Don'ts)
- ❌ **Do not re-color individual segments** with unapproved gradients or foreign colors.
- ❌ **Do not alter the overlapping sequence** of the 3-2-1 ribbons.
- ❌ **Do not squash, stretch, skew, or rotate** the lockup.
- ❌ **Do not place the full-color logo on clashing bright backgrounds** (e.g., bright red or green backgrounds). Always place on clean white, ultra-soft neutral grey, or apply the white inverted logo on high-contrast dark backdrops.
- ❌ **Do not condense the tracking** of "CLEMENTI" — the wide spacing is essential to its structural weight.

## 7. Environmental & UI Applications

### 7.1 Mall Directory & Zone Categorization
The 6 spectrum colors can be systematically mapped across mall levels and experiential zones:
- **Dining & Cafes:** Tangerine Orange (`#F47820`)
- **Cinema & Entertainment:** Coastal Cyan (`#38BEDD`)
- **Fitness & Wellness (Rooftop):** Fresh Leaf Green (`#71BF45`)
- **Fashion & Beauty:** Blossom Pink (`#EF5A92`)
- **Education & Specialty:** Wisteria Lavender (`#A87FB7`)
- **Information & Concierge:** Clementi Crimson (`#ED1651`)

### 7.2 Digital UI Elements
- **Buttons:** Primary interactive buttons use `--clementi-crimson` with white typography and a soft pill or 8px rounded corner (`border-radius: 8px`).
- **Cards & Micro-interactions:** Clean white cards floating on `--color-bg-subtle`, with top-accent border strips corresponding to tenant category colors.
- **Iconography:** Simple 2px line-weight icons matching the primary geometric typography.
- **Barcode Assets:** Code 128 parking voucher barcodes are rendered natively on-device via Canvas and feature an offline Canvas-to-PNG saving utility allowing patrons to download their barcode directly to their device.
